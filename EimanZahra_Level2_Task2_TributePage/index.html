The Journey

Biography

Imran Khan Niazi was born on October 5, 1952, in Lahore, Pakistan. He is a Pakistani politician, former cricketer, and philanthropist who served as the 22nd Prime Minister of Pakistan from August 2018 to April 2022.

Before entering politics, Khan was one of the greatest cricketers of all time. He led Pakistan to its first and only Cricket World Cup victory in 1992. His charismatic leadership and all-rounder skills made him a national hero.

Off the field, Khan founded the Shaukat Khanum Memorial Cancer Hospital & Research Centre in memory of his mother, and later established Namal University in Mianwali, showcasing his commitment to healthcare and education.

In 1996, he founded the political party Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf (PTI), advocating for justice, anti-corruption, and a welfare state. After years of opposition, he became Prime Minister in 2018, focusing on poverty alleviation, healthcare reforms, and foreign policy.

Political Journey

Politics & Vision

Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf (PTI)

Founded in 1996, PTI emerged as a movement for justice and accountability. Imran Khan's vision was to create a "Riasat-e-Madina" - a welfare state based on the principles of justice, equality, and compassion.

Key Political Milestones

  • 1996: Founded PTI in Lahore
  • 2002: First elected as MNA from Mianwali
  • 2013: PTI becomes second-largest party
  • 2018: Elected as 22nd Prime Minister
  • 2019-2022: Launched Ehsaas Program, Sehat Sahulat Program, and 10 Billion Tree Tsunami
